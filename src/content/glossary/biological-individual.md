What counts as a biological individual depends on the question. A physiological inquiry may identify an individual through the integration of metabolism, transport, and regulation. A developmental inquiry may trace the continuity of a developing entity through changes in form. An evolutionary inquiry may identify a reproductive unit or follow a lineage. These criteria can identify different units in clonal plants, colonies, and symbiotic associations.

APS uses **organised persistence** to investigate how a living individual remains continuous through material turnover, development, or disturbance. Regulation, repair, and the reciprocal maintenance of constraints can help explain that continuity where the evidence supports their contribution. The criteria for identifying the individual depend on the form of unity or continuity being investigated.

**Organismality** concerns the extent of physiological integration and self-maintenance characteristic of organisms. **Biological agency** concerns viability-oriented organisational activity. Each attribution requires evidence appropriate to the candidate system and the question being asked.

[**Biological Individuality — Organised Persistence Through Time**](/articles/biological-individuality-organised-persistence-through-time/) develops the account of continuity and its role in explaining biological individuality.
