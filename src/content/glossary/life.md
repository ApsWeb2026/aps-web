In APS, **viability orientation** concerns activity that helps maintain or re-establish conditions compatible with continued functioning. **Constraint closure** concerns the reciprocal dependence of constraints that help sustain the conditions for one another’s operation.

This definition is a canonical commitment of the APS framework. [**What Is Life? — A Biological Question Revisited**](/articles/what-is-life-a-biological-question-revisited/) develops the account, compares it with other approaches, and explains what remains open.
