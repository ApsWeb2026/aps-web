definition: >
  A gene is a genomic region whose activity contributes to the production,
  regulation, or modification of biological processes within a larger
  developmental and organisational context. In APS, genes are not autonomous
  instructions or blueprints but components of viability-oriented systems whose
  effects depend upon the organisation in which they participate.
