## Explanatory Target

Every explanatory architecture is organised around an explanatory target.

The explanatory target identifies the principal biological phenomenon that a research programme seeks to understand. Different organisational research programmes may investigate overlapping biological phenomena while differing in the explanatory targets they regard as central.

Comparative explanatory methodology therefore begins by identifying the explanatory target before examining how that target is explained.
