definition: >
  Regulation is the modification of system activity in ways that maintain,
  restore, or reorganise viability-relevant conditions. In APS, regulation is
  not simply control or response but the ongoing adjustment of activity through
  which living systems sustain organised persistence despite internal and
  external change.

inBrief: >
  Regulation is the maintenance of viability through adaptive adjustment.
