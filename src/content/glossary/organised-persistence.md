A living system can remain continuous through material turnover, development, and environmental change. For example, an organism can retain its continuity while damaged tissue is repaired or remodelled. Organised persistence names the continuity to be explained. In each case, the explanation must show which activities and conditions help sustain it.

**Life** identifies the kind of organisation APS proposes as characteristic of living systems: viability-oriented, constraint-closed organisation. **Biological agency** identifies viability-oriented organisational activity. **Organised persistence** identifies the continuity of living organisation through change.

[**Persistence — Organised Continuity Through Time**](/articles/persistence-organised-continuity-through-time/) develops the fuller account, including how viability, repair, and reorganisation bear on continuity.
