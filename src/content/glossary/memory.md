definition: >
  Memory is the persistence of organisational change through time such that
  past interactions continue to influence future activity. In APS, memory is
  not restricted to conscious recollection or nervous systems but refers more
  generally to the retention of effects that remain causally relevant to
  subsequent regulation, behaviour, development, or adaptation.

inBrief: >
  Memory is the persistence of past influence within present organisation.
