Living systems do not merely possess constraints; they actively organise and reorganise them in response to changing internal and environmental conditions so that the organisation required for continued persistence is maintained.

Constraint Modulation therefore describes how constraint-closed organisation is continually enacted through biological agency. It refers to the ongoing organisation of constraints rather than to isolated regulatory mechanisms or externally imposed control.

## Why It Matters

Constraint Modulation explains why living organisation is an ongoing activity rather than a static state.

Living systems continually experience internal and external change. Persistence therefore depends not simply upon possessing appropriate constraints but upon continually maintaining and re-establishing the organisation those constraints realise.

Constraint Modulation explains how viability-oriented organisation is sustained despite continual material turnover, environmental variation, development, repair, and evolutionary transformation.

It therefore provides the dynamic organisational counterpart to **Constraint Closure**.

## What It Is Not

Constraint Modulation is not equivalent to regulation.

Regulatory mechanisms are particular biological processes that may contribute to Constraint Modulation. Constraint Modulation refers to the broader organisational activity through which living systems continually sustain the constraints required for their own persistence.

Constraint Modulation is not identical with homeostasis.

Homeostasis is one important manifestation of Constraint Modulation but does not exhaust it. Development, repair, adaptation, growth, and many forms of organisational reconfiguration also involve Constraint Modulation whenever they contribute to maintaining viability-oriented organisation.

Constraint Modulation is not externally directed control.

Within APS, the modulation of constraints arises through the organisation of the living system itself rather than being imposed by an external designer, controller, or governing principle.

## Relationship to Other APS Concepts

Constraint Modulation is the ongoing activity through which **Constraint Closure** is maintained over time.

It is enacted through **Biological Agency**, preserves **Biological Organisation**, and continually re-establishes the conditions required for **Viability** and **Organised Persistence**.

Many biological **Functions** contribute to Constraint Modulation by participating in the continual maintenance and adjustment of the constraint network that sustains living organisation.
