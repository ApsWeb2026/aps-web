Describing a biological phenomenon is not the same as explaining it.

Statements such as:
- “the organism adapts”
- “the system survives”
- “the behaviour is goal-directed”

may accurately describe observable patterns without identifying the organisational conditions that make those patterns possible.

APS distinguishes descriptive language from explanatory claims by asking what viability-oriented organisation sustains the phenomenon being described.

Explanation therefore requires more than naming outcomes or behaviours; it requires identifying the organised processes through which living systems persist.
