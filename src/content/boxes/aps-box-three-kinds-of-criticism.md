A **misunderstanding** attributes to APS a claim it does not make.

An **unresolved objection** challenges a claim that APS has not yet adequately demonstrated.

A **negative result** arises when an APS application fails to produce the expected explanatory gain.

Only the first can be resolved by clarification alone.
