Culture is often described as a collection of beliefs, values, traditions, or customs.

APS interprets culture as something more fundamental.

Culture is a socially distributed form of organised persistence.

Through culture, practices, meanings, skills, knowledge, and expectations can persist beyond the lifespan of individual organisms. Continuity becomes historically extended and socially distributed.

Culture therefore functions as a continuity architecture.

Cultural inheritance reproduces that architecture across generations, allowing coordinated activity to persist despite continual turnover among participants.

> Culture is organised persistence operating beyond individual lives.
