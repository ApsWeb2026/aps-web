Life is viability-oriented organised persistence maintained through multiple
interacting continuity architectures.

These architectures include developmental, ecological, evolutionary,
cognitive, and social forms of continuity.

No single process, mechanism, scale, or domain is sufficient to explain life.

Living systems persist because continuity itself is organised.

The organisation of life is therefore the organisation of continuity.
