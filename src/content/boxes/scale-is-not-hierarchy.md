APS treats scale as a way of partitioning and analysing organisation, not as a hierarchy of independently existing ontological levels.

Descriptions at different scales may emphasise:
- molecules
- cells
- tissues
- organisms
- ecological systems

However, these are not separate worlds stacked above one another. They are different perspectives on interconnected organisational processes.

Scale therefore concerns explanatory resolution and organisational perspective rather than ontological rank.
