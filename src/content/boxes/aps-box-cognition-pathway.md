## Cognition in APS

Within APS, cognition is understood as an organisational development emerging from progressively integrated forms of viability-oriented activity.

viability  
↓  
agency  
↓  
evaluation  
↓  
semiosis  
↓  
meaning  
↓  
information  
↓  
representation  
↓  
cognition  
↓  
intelligence  
↓  
consciousness

This sequence does not describe separate substances or independent modules. It describes increasingly integrated organisational developments within living systems.

APS therefore treats:
- cognition as emerging from viability-oriented organisation;
- representation as downstream rather than foundational;
- intelligence as specialised rather than universal;
- and consciousness as one possible development within highly integrated cognition.
