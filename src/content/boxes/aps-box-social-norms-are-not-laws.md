 Social norms and laws both influence behaviour, but they are not the same thing. Laws are formal rules established and enforced through recognised institutions. Social norms may exist without formal enforcement and often operate through shared expectations, approval, criticism, reputation, and collective participation.
>
> Many social norms never become laws, and many laws depend upon existing social norms for their effectiveness. Social norms therefore constitute a broader category of social organisation through which communities coordinate behaviour and maintain collective expectations.
