Different biological questions often highlight different explanatory units.

- **Heredity** — Gene
- **Metabolism and cellular organisation** — Cell
- **Integrated behaviour and regulation** — Organism
- **Evolutionary continuity** — Species or lineage
- **Ecological interaction** — Ecosystem

Each item gives an example of where an explanation might begin. Explaining how an inherited variant affects an organism, for instance, may require tracing its effects on cellular processes and on the organism’s development. Which unit is most informative depends on what the explanation needs to account for.
