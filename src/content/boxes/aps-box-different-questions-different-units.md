Different biological questions often highlight different explanatory units.

| Biological Question | Primary Explanatory Focus |
|---------------------|---------------------------|
| Heredity | Gene |
| Metabolism and cellular organisation | Cell |
| Integrated behaviour and regulation | Organism |
| Evolutionary continuity | Species or lineage |
| Ecological interaction | Ecosystem |

This does not mean that each question belongs exclusively to a single unit. Rather, different biological entities become especially informative for different kinds of explanation. The search for one universally fundamental unit becomes difficult because life itself is organised through multiple interacting forms of continuity.
